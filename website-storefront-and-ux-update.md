# Bamboostic Website: Storefront & Customer Experience Update

**Date:** 22 November 2026  

---

## Overview

We updated the Bamboostic website to position the company clearly as an active, operating business selling products today, while keeping upcoming releases transparent and partnership opportunities accessible. All changes are complete and verified locally.

---

## Key Improvements

### 1. Shifted from an Investor Pitch to a Buyer-First Storefront
* **Home page messaging:** Rewrote the opening banner, capacity section, and bottom call-to-action to focus on homeowners, interior buyers, builders, and bulk buyers rather than fundraising metrics.
* **Customer ordering guide:** Replaced the "Market Signals" section with a clear 3-step **How Ordering Works** guide directly below Featured Products, setting a two-business-day response expectation.
* **Partnerships still accessible:** Moved partnership and investment details into a dedicated section on the About page, with direct links from the home page and footer.

### 2. Simplified Navigation & Page Structure
* **Unified Products tab:** Merged the separate "Products" and "Catalogue" navigation tabs into a single **Products** section, with clear highlighting showing which page the visitor is currently viewing.
* **Stronger About page:** Moved **Who We Serve** (above the company timeline) and the **7-Stage Processing Pipeline** onto the About page so it tells the complete story of who Bamboostic serves and how materials are produced.
* **Cleaner visual layout:** Tightened header spacing, scaled down oversized page titles, removed redundant labels and the white-box footer logo, unified action buttons in Bamboostic's dark forest green, and improved text contrast on dark sections.

### 3. Clearer Product Catalogue & Honest Availability
* **Centralized product catalogue:** Consolidated all 41 products into a single structured catalogue file, making it straightforward to add new items, feature products on the home page, or update availability without code changes.
* **Available vs. Upcoming visibility:** Available items appear first (14 currently available, including 4 of the 6 featured products on the home page), while upcoming releases carry a clear **Upcoming** badge so visitors know what is ready to order now versus coming soon.
* **Left-hand Category filter:** Moved category filters into a clean left sidebar so products appear immediately at the top of the page.

### 4. New Enquiry List ("No-Checkout") Ordering Flow
* **Add to Enquiry List:** Visitors can add any available or upcoming product to an Enquiry List from product cards or detail pages, with no quantities, pricing tiers, or credit card required.
* **Pre-drafted Contact form:** Opening the Enquiry List takes visitors straight to the Contact page with the form immediately visible at the top, their selected products listed, **Product Order / Quote** pre-selected, and an editable enquiry email already drafted.
* **Email delivery note:** Until automated web email delivery (Resend) is connected, submitting the form opens a pre-filled email draft addressed to `info@nabattansprings.com` in the visitor's email app.

### 5. Natural, Human Copy
* Removed stiff phrasing, "concept" labels on active products, and all em-dashes across the site and product descriptions so the copy reads naturally.
